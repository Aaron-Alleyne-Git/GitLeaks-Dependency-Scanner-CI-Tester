# CI/CD pipeline

The pipeline has 13 phases. This document covers the first two.

| Phase | Stage(s) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lint` | Spell check for docs |
| 2 | `security`, `gate` | Secrets scan, dependency audits, and the G8 gate |
| 3 to 13 | `build` onwards | Image build, tests, deploy (documented separately) |

## Runner

Jobs run on the shell runner tagged `pulse-shell`. The tools installed on it are
listed in `ci/rules.yml` (`RUNNER_TOOLS`). A job can only use a tool that is on
that list.

## Phase 2: scanners and the G8 gate

The scanner jobs write reports to `reports/` and never fail on findings. The
`g8-gate` job reads the reports and decides.

| Scanner job | Tool | Runs | Report |
|---|---|---|---|
| `secrets-scan` | gitleaks | Every pipeline | `reports/gitleaks.json` |
| `composer-audit` | `composer audit --locked` | Nightly, and MRs that change a `composer.lock` | `reports/composer-audit.json` |
| `npm-audit` | `npm audit` | Nightly, and MRs that change `package-lock.json` | `reports/npm-audit.json` |

G8 fails the pipeline when any of these is true:

1. A scanner report is missing.
2. gitleaks has an open finding.
3. `ci/dependency-exceptions.json` breaks its own policy.
4. A high or critical Composer advisory has no live exception.
5. npm reports a high or critical vulnerability.
6. The counts table below is out of date.

### Dependency exceptions

An advisory that cannot be fixed before the next nightly can be recorded in
`ci/dependency-exceptions.json`. The limits are in the file's `policy` block:

- At most 5 active exceptions.
- A critical advisory can be excepted for at most 14 days, a high one for at most 30.
- Every entry needs the advisory ID, package, path, severity, a reason of at
  least 30 characters, an owner, a ticket, and `added` and `expires` dates.

When an exception expires, the advisory blocks again.
